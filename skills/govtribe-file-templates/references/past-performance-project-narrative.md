# Past Performance Project Narrative

Use after selecting `past-performance-project-narrative`. If `template-library.md` is not beside this guide, no template is installed: create the sections under **Without a template** natively and ignore instructions to inspect or copy a template.

1. **Read the template before drafting.** Inspect the selected Word file, including every section and table prompt; the detailed completion instructions are inside the template. Follow the buyer's required form, order and limits where they differ.
2. **Reuse or select project evidence.** Use **Past Performance Match** (`govtribe-capture-workflows` → `references/past-performance-match.md`, from that skill's installed root) for existing User Files, pursuit context, project selection and relevance research.
3. **Complete and check the narrative.** Replace drafting prompts with supported project content and remove inapplicable optional sections. Use [Template adaptation and revision checks](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md) for native file creation, revision, saved-page inspection and delivery.

## Without a template

Create an editable document titled with the project name and "Past performance project narrative", showing the target buyer, requirement, solicitation and evaluation factor. Open with two or three sentences on the customer's need, the work your company performed and the strongest supported result. Then include:

- **Project snapshot:** customer and buying office; contract or order identifier, performing legal entity and role; performance period and whether completed or ongoing as of a date; clearly labeled contract value, your company's share and relevant delivered quantities.
- **Relevance to this requirement:** comparable scope, scale, complexity, tasks and quantities beside the cited requirement; the applicable recency rule; material differences or limits. Distinguish your company's work from a prime's, partner's or predecessor's, and do not assign an evaluator rating.
- **Work performed:** your company's own tasks, deliverables, methods and constraints, and the customer's role, separated from the wider contract scope.
- **Results and performance:** two or three supported outcomes, each metric with value, unit, measurement period, baseline or denominator and supporting record; precise schedule and financial performance, including any missed milestone or scope change. For ongoing work, report only results achieved as of the stated date.
- **Challenges and corrective actions:** a material problem, its effect, the corrective action and the observed outcome. Remove this section if nothing relevant needs discussion and the buyer does not require it.
- **Customer reference:** name, title, organization, authorized contact details and direct relationship to the work. Confirm contact details before use.
- **Supporting evidence:** the contract, delivery, acceptance or performance records behind the claims, and the controlling requirement's location.

Follow the buyer's required form, order, recency rule and total submission limit, and remove unsupported claims.
