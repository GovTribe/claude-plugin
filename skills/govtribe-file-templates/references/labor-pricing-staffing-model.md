# Labor Pricing & Staffing

Use after resolving `labor-pricing-staffing-model` to build or revise an editable labor-cost and pricing workbook.

1. **Read the model and establish the pricing basis.** Inspect Summary, Assumptions, Staffing, Labor Rates, Pricing and Source Data. Reuse the buyer's pricing instructions, staffing requirements, company assumptions and selected working revision. The master has six role slots, a base plus four options, and a separate extension; adapt the working copy if the required structure differs. Identify the buyer, project, location, pricing date, periods and requested decision. Identify any required nonlabor charges or buyer-specific evaluation totals that need an expanded model. Use the buyer's original pricing form when required; the library example supplies no market or company evidence.

2. **Complete Source Data and reuse Pricing research.** Enter labor categories, qualifications, estimated positions, requested hours by site/period and their document/page or cell sources. Reconcile site totals before combining roles. From `govtribe-pricing-data`'s installed root, use **Staffing and Labor Costs** (`references/staffing-wage-and-labor-category-workflow.md`) for unresolved workload, wage, location and labor-category questions. Record each wage's occupation/proxy, geography, level, data year and retrieval date. Use **Comparable Prices and Cost Drivers** (`references/comparable-prices-and-cost-drivers.md`) when testing a rate against outside evidence. Keep wages, published ceilings, awarded rates and proposed prices distinct; sufficient supplied inputs need no fresh research.

3. **Set periods and check staffing capacity.** Enter consecutive start/end dates and each period's actual requested hours. In Assumptions, set annual paid hours and nonbillable hours from the proposed employment model. Staffing converts those into required paid FTEs and available billable hours, accounting for period duration. Estimated positions, paid FTEs and named people can differ. Check required posts, shifts and relief coverage separately from the FTE total. Use a proposed-FTE override only for an intentional staffing plan, explain it, and resolve any capacity shortfall. Do not count paid leave both as nonbillable time and fringe.

4. **Choose each role's rate basis and cost assumptions.** With **Build from wage**, enter the benchmark wage/year, applicable escalation, fringe, additional hourly benefits, overhead, G&A and profit assumptions. Read the template's allocation bases: fringe applies to paid labor, overhead to labor plus benefits, and G&A to that subtotal plus overhead. Confirm these match the company model before using the result. Select **Profit on cost** or **Margin on sales** deliberately. With **Use supplied loaded rate**, enter the supplied selling rate and its source; it feeds price directly and does not establish underlying cost or profit. Do not burden it again. Put a negotiated period rate in its override cell rather than replacing a formula.

5. **Evaluate the relevant assumptions.** Use Assumptions' single Low/Base/High selector and populate the cases needed for the question; do not invent values merely to fill every case. Check wage-year adjustment and later-period escalation separately from supplied-rate escalation. A period override changes that period; the extension defaults to Option 4's final rate and cost assumptions while retaining separate hours and duration. Explain material staffing, rate or margin differences and unresolved inputs. Use **Build, Review and Revise a Pricing Model** (`references/pricing-model-workflow.md`) for economics and scoped revisions, and **Price-to-Win Research and Modeling** (`references/price-to-win-analysis.md`) when assessing competitiveness.

6. **Reconcile and test the saved workbook.** Independently check representative hours, paid FTEs, loaded costs, selling rates, profit and period totals. Reconcile Pricing to Summary; keep the extension separate from the five-year total and preserve unavailable cost/profit where only selling rates are known. In disposable copies, change a material wage, staffing input, later-period assumption or rate override and confirm affected detail and summaries update. Check selected versus unselected missing assumptions and valid zeros. Preserve formulas and controls, inspect every saved sheet for readable labels and values, and use [Template adaptation and revision checks](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md) for visual review and delivery.
