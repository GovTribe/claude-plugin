# System / Program Research

Use after resolving `system-program-research` to build an editable research workbook for the user's program, system or market-entry question.

1. **Read the workbook and define the target.** Inspect Overview, System Profile, Contracts, Funding, Timeline and Research Log, including grouped input columns and formulas. Start from the verified master or selected working revision; the populated example illustrates the format. Establish the exact program, system or variant, responsible agency, research cutoff and award issue-date window. Distinguish the broader program from the system being assessed.

2. **Reuse Market Intelligence research and register sources.** From `govtribe-market-intelligence`'s installed root, use **Defense Program and Supplier Research** (`references/defense-program-and-supplier-research.md`) for program identity, suppliers and awarded work, and **Federal Contract and Vehicle Research** (`references/federal-contract-and-vehicle-research.md`) for ordering routes and contract history. For civilian systems, use the selected agency/program records and official system material without requiring a defense-program ID. Reuse existing findings. Enter each source once in Research Log with its ID, URL, publication/retrieval dates, covered records or pages and material limitations; reference those IDs in the other sheets.

3. **Populate System Profile and Contracts.** Explain mission, users, responsible offices, current phase, supported functions, architecture and recent changes using dated sources. Match technical claims to the selected version/configuration and distinguish confirmed facts, attributed supplier claims, inferences and unknowns. For each reviewed award, retain its record ID, contract reference, legal awardee/UEI, scope, current and ultimate ends, parent IDV and links. Check that each award link matches its record identifier; product descriptions support capabilities, not award amounts or dates. Assess system relevance separately from a program code or similar title, recording the rationale. Describe the work actually awarded before calling a vendor an operational incumbent. Keep uncertain and out-of-scope records visible without counting them as in-scope obligations. Those obligations are cumulative at retrieval, not spending during the award issue-date window; avoid overlapping parent/order totals.

4. **Complete Funding and Timeline.** Use **Federal Budget Data** (`references/federal-budget-data.md`) to select matching program lines and fiscal years appropriate to the question, using one publication vintage for the main comparison. Enter original amounts and units so the workbook converts them to USD millions; label actual, enacted and requested amounts accurately. Keep contract obligations separate, missing amounts distinct from zero, and explain what the selected lines cover. Use **Federal Procurement Status and History** (`references/federal-procurement-status-and-history.md`) for forecasts and notices. Record completed events, planned milestones and unresolved timing, retaining quarter/year precision when no exact date exists. Distinguish performance ends, ordering deadlines and response deadlines; verify current access rather than inferring it from an incumbent or historical vehicle.

5. **Explain the findings and remaining questions.** Write Overview's current position, access/timing and confirmation needs for the reader's decision, citing the supporting source IDs. In Research Log, connect each material question to its decision impact, next research action and timing. Mark it resolved only when the recorded evidence answers it. Preserve unknown technical, supplier or procurement details rather than turning gaps into negative findings.

6. **Check the saved workbook.** Reconcile reviewed-award counts, in-scope obligations, funding conversions and open questions against the source rows. Resolve unexpected input-check messages and identify missing source inputs; preserve formulas and extend tables, validations and chart ranges when adding rows. In a disposable copy, change an amount, relevance or question status and confirm affected summaries update. Inspect all saved sheets and the funding chart for clipping, missing sources and placeholder text. Use [Template adaptation and revision checks](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md) for visual inspection and delivery of the editable workbook.
