# Teaming Partner Shortlist

Use after resolving `teaming-partner-shortlist` to compare potential partners for a specific pursuit.

1. **Read the workbook.** Inspect all six sheets, their instructions and formulas before filling the verified blank master or selected working revision. Blue cells are inputs; gray cells calculate. Expand grouped columns to find the candidate, need and source IDs. The populated example illustrates the method; its prime capabilities and recommendations are not inputs for yours.

2. **Define the pursuit and teaming needs.** Complete the Shortlist header with buyer, pursuit, prime, solicitation, as-of date and any published submission deadline, time zone and format. In Teaming Needs, record the prime's supported capabilities and proposed teaming structure, then identify buyer requirements, Required or Preferred priority, and the gaps partners must fill. Separate confirmed gaps from capabilities that remain unverified. Reuse current capture research and controlling amendments.

3. **Gather candidate evidence.** Use these references from `govtribe-capture-workflows`' installed root as needed: **Likely Bidders** (`references/likely-bidders.md`) for complementary-company discovery; **Past Performance Match** (`references/past-performance-match.md`) for relevant awards, existing User Files, pursuit context and actual workshare; **Team On An Opportunity** (`references/team-on-an-opportunity.md`) for GovTribe interests, matches and conversations. Reuse existing findings. Register each source once in Sources with its date, retrieval date, URL and limitations. Link each candidate to its own legal-entity source and UEI; leave unavailable identity evidence unresolved.

4. **Map capabilities and relevant work.** Give candidates and needs stable IDs. In Capability Match, assess each candidate against the required needs with Pass, Fail or Unresolved, a specific explanation, source/location and what still needs confirmation. Keep gaps outside a specialist's proposed role visible and explain which other team member would cover them. In Relevant Work, record the customer, actual performing role, scope, performance dates, relevance and limitations. Its `Work reference` column holds the candidate ID. Preserve the distinction between awarded scope and demonstrated results.

5. **Check conditions and recommend partners.** In Checks & Actions, record pursuit-specific eligibility, vehicle access, capacity, availability, conflicts or workshare questions, with status, action and internal due date. Use `TEAM` only for checks applying to every candidate. A completed check needs confirmation evidence; a buyer requirement alone does not confirm the candidate meets it. Apply confirmed eligibility/access constraints before choosing Shortlist, Research further or Exclude. Give a reason, proposed role, main condition and next action. A shortlist can be conditional; distinguish expressed interest from committed staff, availability and agreed teaming terms.

6. **Check the saved workbook.** Reconcile candidate identities, need/source links, recommendations and outstanding actions. Preserve formulas and validation lists; extend tables and dependent ranges together when needed. Recalculate and inspect the summary, required-check counts, dates and input messages. These counts summarize recorded gaps, not a candidate ranking or proof that a team is complete. Use [Template adaptation and revision checks](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md) for saved-sheet inspection and delivery.
