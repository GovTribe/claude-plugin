# Proposal Response

Use after selecting `proposal-response` for a new response or continuing a selected proposal draft. Use [Template adaptation and revision checks](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md) for native file work and delivery in GTAI or the connected host.

1. **Establish the scope and requirement shred.** Identify the requested proposal, volume, section, revision or review and its deliverables. Distinguish buyer instructions, prior proposals, supporting evidence and the selected draft. Before drafting a full response, complete or refresh **Solicitation Intelligence and Compliance Extraction** (`govtribe-proposal-workflows`, `references/solicitation-intelligence-and-compliance-extraction.md`) from its installed root. Reuse a current requirement register and preserve IDs and source locators. Stop full-response drafting if missing, unreadable or conflicting controlling sources leave material requirements unresolved; identify the specific gap. For scoped requests, cover the applicable requirements, including package-wide instructions.

2. **Organize the response for the buyer.** Map each instruction, evaluation factor and question to its response section, supporting evidence and remaining input. Follow the buyer's order, terminology, volumes, page limits and formatting rules. If a response structure is needed, build an annotated outline from the requirement map before drafting. Adapt the master's headings to this procurement, retaining only applicable sections. Keep technical, past-performance and price material in their required files. Distinguish supporting company exhibits from required forms and enclosures; use buyer-provided originals at the controlling revision. Reuse a prior proposal's useful structure or wording where requested, while checking its scope and claims against the new solicitation.

3. **Draft the actual solution.** Use `govtribe-govcon-writing` reference `references/proposal-narratives.md` from its installed root. Explain how the work will be done: activities and sequence, responsible roles, resources, deliverables and acceptance or quality controls. Develop requested staffing, management or transition plans around the supplied workload and service boundaries. Connect supported differentiators to the stated evaluation factors. A requested proposal or volume needs substantive answers, beyond an outline or compliance summary. For a strategy-only request, provide the response plan instead. Distinguish proposed methods and staffing from established practices, committed personnel and existing approvals.

4. **Reuse company evidence and select past performance.** Reuse selected evidence. When research is needed, follow **Past Performance Match** (`govtribe-capture-workflows`, `references/past-performance-match.md`) from its installed root for existing User Files, relevant pursuit context and project selection before requesting more material. Use [Past Performance Project Narrative](past-performance-project-narrative.md) to prepare selected examples. Match the buyer's reference count, recency, scope, performer roles and questionnaire rules. Preserve prime, subcontractor and individual attribution. Build resumes from supplied qualifications within the buyer's limits. Match security claims to the actual system/data boundary. Keep missing credentials, commitments and signatures visible; template examples are fictional.

5. **Revise the selected package consistently.** Start from the user's chosen revision and retain accepted answers, artwork and unaffected terms. When asked to restore earlier content, compare the identified versions section by section and reuse supported completed answers before marking information missing. Apply amendments and factual corrections across affected narratives, resumes, staffing tables, forms and cross-references. Preserve material qualifications when shortening. For review-only requests, identify the affected section, controlling requirement, discrepancy and suggested correction; leave the files unchanged unless edits were requested.

6. **Reconcile and deliver the requested files.** Check required answers, forms, acknowledgments, signatures, deadline and submission instructions against the current requirement register. Align staffing, hours, workshare, transition, quantities and assumptions across technical and price files; use Pricing for unresolved delivery economics. Check each volume's final rendered page limit and required format, including resumes and attachments under the buyer's counting rules. Follow the adaptation reference to inspect the saved files and deliver matching editable sources and requested exports. Identify any replacement text still needing insertion, and summarize material gaps separately from government-facing copy.
