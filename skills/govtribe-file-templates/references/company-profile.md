# Company Profile & Due Diligence

Use after resolving `company-profile` to prepare a sourced company assessment for the reader's decision.

1. **Read the template and define the scope.** Inspect its instructions, tables, source list and headers before filling the verified blank master or selected working revision. Establish the reader's purpose, exact legal entity, related entities included and research date. The example illustrates the format; its company facts are not inputs for this profile.

2. **Reuse Market Intelligence research.** From `govtribe-market-intelligence`'s installed root, use **Company Profiles and Diligence** (`references/company-profiles-and-diligence.md`) for identity, ownership, capabilities and business relationships. Use **Federal Buying Pattern Analysis** (`references/federal-buying-pattern-analysis.md`) for customers, prime awards, grants, subcontracts and spending comparisons, and **Federal Contract and Vehicle Research** (`references/federal-contract-and-vehicle-research.md`) for material contract or vehicle details. Reuse existing findings and fill the gaps relevant to this assessment.

3. **Complete the company snapshot and capabilities.** Identify the legal name, UEI/CAGE, location and as-of date; distinguish registered, mailing and operating addresses when relevant. Explain what the company does, its principal government customers and why it matters to the reader. Fill the snapshot with useful registration, business-contact and ownership facts, retaining record update dates. Reconcile conflicting registration or certification claims by source and date, and flag what remains unresolved. Describe capabilities with supporting examples and attribute company-reported claims. Distinguish the assessed entity from its parent, affiliates and similarly named businesses.

4. **Select representative government work.** For each relevant award or project, record the customer, identifier, actual prime/subcontractor role, awarded scope and dated evidence. Label award dates, performance periods, recorded completion and acceptance accurately. Where amounts matter, state their period and basis; separate obligations, ceilings, options and company financial figures. Keep grants and subcontracts distinct from prime contracts and avoid overlapping parent/subsidiary totals. Adapt the table to the evidence available rather than filling every example row; explain search coverage when no relevant work is found.

5. **Explain contract access and relationships.** Name the legal holder and the company's role for each vehicle, IDV, joint venture or partner route. Record current ordering limits and source dates; distinguish vehicle access from orders won. Explain material ownership or relationship changes using dated evidence. Shared customers or an affiliate's vehicle do not establish this company's own access or partnership. State search coverage when access is unverified.

6. **Finish the assessment and inspect the saved document.** Connect the findings to the reader's purpose and use Questions to resolve for material uncertainties and the specific evidence needed; distinguish unknowns from adverse findings. Match citations to a dated Sources entry with a usable URL. Replace instructions, unused rows and placeholder branding; reconcile identity, dates, amounts and links throughout. Use [Template adaptation and revision checks](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md) for saved-page inspection and delivery.
