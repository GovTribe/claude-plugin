# RFQ Quotation Response

Use after selecting `rfq-quotation-response` or when continuing a selected quotation. Use [Template adaptation and revision checks](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md) for native file work and delivery.

1. **Establish the requested quotation.** Reuse the current RFQ or call order, amendments, Q&A, statement of work, pricing schedule, forms and requirement map. Confirm buyer and offeror identities, required deliverables and file formats, deadline/time zone, submission channel, page limits and price-evaluation rules. A requested price sheet may be the entire deliverable. If the package needs extraction, use `govtribe-proposal-workflows` reference `references/solicitation-intelligence-and-compliance-extraction.md` from its installed root. Reconcile current call-specific pricing instructions with the standing agreement; flag conflicts rather than silently substituting older terms.

2. **Define the offered solution.** Answer the technical requirements using supported company evidence and `govtribe-govcon-writing` for narrative. Tie each priced line to its exact product, model, configuration or service scope, CLIN/part identifier, quantity, unit and performance period. Address required delivery, acceptance, training and support. Distinguish the firm offer from alternatives or substitutions, and retain any required buyer-approval condition. An assumed approval in a scenario is not an approval already received. Follow buyer order and omit unnecessary master sections.

3. **Build the price schedule.** Use approved offered prices and commercial inputs; use `govtribe-pricing-data` for needed rate or cost research. Keep unrequested internal cost and margin analysis outside the buyer-facing offer. For each line, show the required unit rate and extension. Separate recurring charges from one-time delivery, installation or other fees. Apply approved discounts, escalation and rounding consistently; reconcile base, option and evaluated totals under the buyer's inclusion rule. An evaluated option is not automatically a base commitment. Keep invoice frequency separate from calculation basis: annual billing may still require monthly rates.

4. **Make the pricing basis explicit.** Distinguish actual offered prices from applicable ceiling rates, checking ceilings on comparable units and tax bases. State currency, tax inclusion and required surcharges consistently. If a second currency is requested, identify the controlling currency, exchange rate and its supplied date/basis; label reference conversions. Keep a possible later tax refund separate from the quoted total. Reconcile every price sheet, quotation summary and commercial schedule to the same basis.

5. **Complete terms and buyer forms.** Populate the buyer's price sheet, SF1449 or other required forms using [buyer-form guidance](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md). Preserve their fields and formulas. Confirm delivery location/basis, validity, payment terms, warranty and exceptions from the supplied instructions and approved inputs. Keep scheduled dates distinct from an award-triggered duration. Leave unsupported prices, certifications, commitments and signer actions unresolved; keep internal completion notes separate.

6. **Check the selected revision.** Apply the requested changes to the selected file, retaining accepted wording and prices elsewhere. Recalculate affected lines and dependent totals, then compare the technical offer, quantities, prices, delivery, conditions and terms across all deliverables. Check required answers, forms, signatures and amendment acknowledgments against the current requirement map. Use the exact sourced deadline rather than substituting “close of business.” Follow the adaptation reference to inspect and deliver matching editable files and requested exports; identify any remaining inputs and keep examples labeled as illustrative.
