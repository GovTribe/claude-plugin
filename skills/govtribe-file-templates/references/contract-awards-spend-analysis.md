# Contract Awards & Spend Analysis

Use after resolving `contract-awards-spend-analysis` to build an editable workbook for the user's market or buyer decision.

1. **Read the workbook and set the scope.** Inspect Dashboard, Trends, Vendors & Buyers, Awards and Transactions, including grouped input/calculation columns, dropdowns and chart sources. Use the verified master or selected working revision; the NSF example illustrates the format, not current research. Establish buyer, funding versus contracting agency, work categories, date window and retrieval date. For a supplied list or working workbook, preserve its cohort, identifiers and requested field locations. For federal spending, define fiscal years by transaction dates—October 1 through September 30—including actions on older awards.

2. **Reuse Market Intelligence research.** From `govtribe-market-intelligence`'s installed root, use **Federal Buying Pattern Analysis** (`references/federal-buying-pattern-analysis.md`) for buyer/vendor scope, complete search retrieval, aggregation and comparable periods. Use **Federal Contract and Vehicle Research** (`references/federal-contract-and-vehicle-research.md`) for parent/order relationships, modifications and value definitions. Reuse existing findings; retrieve signed [federal transactions](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-federal-transactions-mcp-tool) for spending over time and [contract awards](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-federal-contract-awards-mcp-tool) for current award details. An award inventory alone cannot establish period-spend trends. Keep grants, downstream subawards and shared vehicle ceilings outside prime-contract totals.

3. **Prepare traceable source rows.** Keep one row per transaction ID and one snapshot per award ID; retain award, vendor and office IDs, original names, UEIs and source links. Deduplicate by record ID, not company name or contract number alone. Preserve signed amounts, real zero actions and unknown values separately. Record the query, pages/windows retrieved, missing records, exclusions and duplicate handling in the workbook. Reconcile the normalized transaction total with a same-scope search aggregate when available; explain differences instead of treating a retrieved page or shortlist as the whole market.

4. **Populate the connected sheets.** Enter scope and coverage in Transactions; fill Awards with dated snapshots and Vendors & Buyers with the matching vendor/office registries, then load the transaction inputs. Keep period obligations, covered obligations, current award obligations and potential ceilings distinct. Preserve calculated columns and refresh table ranges, fiscal-year/quarter rows, category lists, dropdown sources and chart bindings to cover the actual data. Include explicit unknown categories where supported by the source. Resolve input-check messages; document unavailable inputs that prevent a complete result.

5. **Build the requested comparisons.** Set Dashboard's fiscal year, vendor, funding office and award-type filters. Reconcile its KPIs, rankings and breakdowns with Trends and the selected transaction rows. Annual/quarterly history spans the covered years; the ledger retains its source rows and award snapshots remain current. Compare matching complete periods, or label partial coverage; distinguish positive obligations, negative deobligations and net obligations. Count distinct awards/vendors with nonzero actions separately from transaction count. Explain concentration and the awards driving change. Current set-asides do not measure historical small-business participation; performance location is not vendor headquarters.

6. **Check the saved workbook and deliver.** Confirm totals, counts, units, source links and coverage; preserve meaningful missing values instead of displaying them as zero. Test a filter change and a representative source-value change in a disposable copy, recalculate and confirm dependent tables/charts update. Inspect every saved sheet, charts and print areas for clipping or omitted rows, replacing placeholder branding and instructions. Use [Template adaptation and revision checks](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md) for visual inspection and delivery of the editable workbook.
