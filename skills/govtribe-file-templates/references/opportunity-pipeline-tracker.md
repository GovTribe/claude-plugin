# Opportunity Pipeline Tracker

Use after resolving `opportunity-pipeline-tracker` to build or refresh an editable pursuit portfolio and action tracker.

1. **Read the workbook and define the portfolio.** Inspect Dashboard, Pipeline, Actions and Source Data, including grouped columns and the settings below the source table. Use the verified blank master or selected working revision. Establish the company, portfolio owner, scope and review date; set Dashboard's **As of** date. Preserve the user's pursuit IDs and requested cohort. The example illustrates the layout, not current opportunities or company judgments.

2. **Reuse Capture research and register sources.** From `govtribe-capture-workflows`' installed root, use **Review My Pipeline** (`references/review-my-pipeline.md`) to retrieve pursuits, linked records, tasks and relevant discussions. Use **Relevant Opportunities** (`references/relevant-opportunities.md`) when asked to find additions, and **Manage Pipelines and Pursuits** (`references/workspace-pipeline-operations.md`) for workspace organization or requested updates. Register external records in Source Data with stable source and pursuit IDs, original titles, GovTribe IDs/URLs, source versions, retrieval dates and evidence. Keep a forecast and its later solicitation under one pursuit when they represent the same acquisition. Select the controlling source ID on Pipeline; its linked pursuit ID must agree. Preserve original deadline text, time and time zone, and retain quarter-only forecasts as periods rather than invented dates.

3. **Populate internal pursuit judgments.** Map the team's actual stages to Source Data's **StageMap**, retaining each stage's active/closed flag; government notice status is a separate field. Enter the pursuit owner, response type, company estimate, value basis and period, probability of win, notes and review date. Keep the government's opportunity range separate from the company's expected contract value; annual amounts and vehicle ceilings are not total company value. The dashboard includes only active estimates labeled **Total company contract value** with a value period. Enter probabilities as percentages: a GovTribe value of 30 becomes 30% (`0.30`) in the workbook, and record its basis in Notes. Leave unknown estimates or probabilities blank; zero means a known zero. Preserve won, lost and abandoned history without including it in active totals.

4. **Record the work needed to advance each pursuit.** Populate Actions with stable action and pursuit IDs, a concrete next action, owner, due date, status and any result or blocker. Map status names to the completion flags in **WorkStatuses**. Reconcile completed dates with status; retain completed work and explain undated open actions. Pipeline and Dashboard calculate next actions from these rows. Keep source refreshes separate from internal decisions, and use the Capture workflow when workspace changes are requested.

5. **Review the portfolio and refresh deliberately.** Check active counts, estimated and probable value, and the displayed valuation coverage. Missing probabilities can make probable value incomplete. The stage chart includes closed history; active KPIs do not. For outcomes within a reporting period, use dated stage changes rather than current closed counts. The response window includes the As-of date and excludes the date 30 days later. Check overdue actions, passed response dates and pursuits needing review; a passed deadline alone does not mean the pursuit was lost. Set the review interval in Source Data. Refresh records without overwriting pursuit judgments, then update **Last reviewed** and **Reviewed source version** only after reviewing the changes.

6. **Check the saved workbook.** Resolve duplicate IDs, broken source links and input warnings; explain material unavailable inputs. Add rows inside the Excel tables and extend calculated columns, dependent ranges, validations and print areas together. Independently reconcile representative value/probability calculations and active, deadline and action counts. In disposable copies, change a value, probability, stage, date or action completion and confirm the linked detail, summaries and chart respond correctly. Inspect every saved sheet and all requested printed rows for clipping or omissions. Use [Template adaptation and revision checks](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md) for visual review and delivery.
