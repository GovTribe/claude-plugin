# RFI / Sources Sought Response

Use after selecting `rfi-sources-sought-response` or when continuing a selected response. Use [Template adaptation and revision checks](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md) for native file work and delivery.

1. **Establish the requested response.** Reuse the current notice, amendments, questionnaire, information sheet and existing requirement map. Identify the actual questions, requested attachments, deadline/time zone, submission channel and page or word limits, including whether covers and attachments count. If the package needs extraction, use `govtribe-proposal-workflows` reference `references/solicitation-intelligence-and-compliance-extraction.md` from its installed root. Distinguish a capability statement, answers for a portal and a required buyer form; one may accompany another.

2. **Establish the company's role.** Determine whether the company is responding as a prime, subcontractor, OEM/product owner, reseller or implementation/support partner. Use supplied company and teaming evidence; clarify the intended role if it changes the answers and remains unclear. Separate work the company performs itself, work an identified partner would perform and arrangements still being proposed. Keep the government buyer, respondent, prime and product owner distinct.

3. **Answer each question from that role.** Preserve the buyer's question numbers and order. Give the direct answer first, followed by the relevant capability, delivery approach, capacity or acquisition feedback requested. For example, an implementation partner can explain migration, integration, training and support without claiming to own the product or its authorization. Identify product-specific answers that need the OEM's evidence. Describe proposed teaming as proposed, and use `govtribe-govcon-writing` for prose. Follow the RFI's questions and requested depth.

4. **Connect experience to the requirement.** Choose relevant projects from the supplied evidence. For each, write a short paragraph identifying the customer or program, period, the company's actual role, work performed, supported results and relevance to the buyer's need. Distinguish the company's contribution from the prime's overall work; distinguish staff experience from corporate performance where applicable. Reuse exact supported metrics and confirm requested identifiers, certifications and personnel status. Leave missing facts visible in the working draft rather than borrowing the template example's claims.

5. **Assemble the requested package.** Continue the selected revision and accepted language. Populate the response and required surveys or information sheets using the [buyer-form guidance](template-adaptation-and-revisions.md). For a portal, prepare answers against the available field labels and limits. If linked questions or attachments cannot be accessed, identify the missing source and the answers it prevents completing; retain useful work from the available questions. Include separate supporting attachments only as requested, and place internal completion questions outside buyer-facing answers.

6. **Check the response against the request.** Compare each question and required attachment with the assembled package. Check the respondent's role, project claims, identifiers and numbers across the narrative and forms, then check the current submission instructions and limits. Use the shared adaptation reference to inspect the saved files and deliver the response, companion answers and any internal notes with their purposes clear.
