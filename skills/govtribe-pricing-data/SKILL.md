# GovTribe Pricing Data

Before your first GovTribe search, read [Choose a search mode and write queries](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/guides/choose-a-search-mode-and-write-queries), unless already in context. Alternatively, retrieve it with `Documentation` using `collections=["govtribe-for-agents"]` and `article_names=["Choose a search mode and write queries"]`.

## Research workflows

Read the matching workflow references below before relevant calculations or price judgments, including when all inputs are supplied. For combined labor and price questions, read **Staffing and Labor Costs** and **Comparable Prices and Cost Drivers**; when judging a proposed bid, carry their findings through **Price-to-Win Research and Modeling**. Reuse the active opportunity, files, proposed price and assumptions, including for terse follow-ups such as `ptw`.

- **What should we bid, or is our proposed price defensible?** [Price-to-Win Research and Modeling](references/price-to-win-analysis.md).
- **What staffing and labor costs are reasonable?** [Staffing and Labor Costs](references/staffing-wage-and-labor-category-workflow.md) covers workload, FTEs, salaries, loaded rates and wrap assumptions.
- **What have buyers paid for comparable work?** [Comparable Prices and Cost Drivers](references/comparable-prices-and-cost-drivers.md) selects price evidence, units and scope adjustments for labor, products, subscriptions, construction and other services.
- **Build, review or revise a pricing workbook:** [Build, Review and Revise a Pricing Model](references/pricing-model-workflow.md) covers labor and nonlabor economics, formulas, fee bases, sensitivity checks and selected revisions.

## Supporting references

- **Pricing sources:** [BLS wages](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/bls-occupational-wage-data-mcp-tool), [GSA labor ceilings](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-gsa-labor-rates-mcp-tool), [Service Contract Inventory](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-service-contract-inventory-mcp-tool) and [awarded line items](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-line-items-mcp-tool).
- **Existing pricing files:** [Search_User_Files](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-user-files-mcp-tool) locates requested prior work; [source-content retrieval](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/guides/vector-store-content-retrieval) retrieves passages. Use native file tools for originals and formulas.

Use `govtribe-file-templates` for template masters and revision checks, and `govtribe-govcon-writing` for pricing narrative. Use `govtribe-capture-workflows` for bid/no-bid and pursuit decisions, and `govtribe-proposal-workflows` for buyer submission requirements.
