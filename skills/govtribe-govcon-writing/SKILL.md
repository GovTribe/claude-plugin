# GovCon Writing

## Writing routes

Choose the references matching the requested work. Reuse the current buyer, evidence, selected draft and accepted wording. For revisions, change the requested passages and preserve the rest; for review-only requests, return findings.

- **Write a technical, management, staffing or quality-control approach:** [Proposal narratives](references/proposal-narratives.md).
- **Tailor a capability statement or turn project experience into persuasive past performance:** [Proposal narratives](references/proposal-narratives.md). Match the buyer and the company's actual role.
- **Answer an RFI, sources-sought notice or market-research questionnaire:** [RFI and sources-sought responses](references/rfi-and-sources-sought-responses.md).
- **Fit an answer into a survey field or shorten it without losing meaning:** [RFI and sources-sought responses](references/rfi-and-sources-sought-responses.md) for field limits; use the relevant narrative or correspondence guide for other revisions.
- **Draft a cover letter, submission email or contracting-officer question:** [Official correspondence](references/official-correspondence.md).

Produce the requested words from available context; a capability statement or email does not require a solicitation. For missing solicitation requirements, use `govtribe-proposal-workflows` reference `references/solicitation-intelligence-and-compliance-extraction.md` from its installed root. Reuse an existing requirement map.

## Supporting references

- [Source authority and claims](references/source-authority-and-claims.md): conflicting requirements, missing facts, performer attribution and proposed commitments.
- [State and local adaptation](references/state-and-local-adaptation.md): buyer-specific terminology, forms and addenda.
- [Research sources](references/research-sources.md): official guidance when a procedural rule needs verification.
- **Create or revise a complete file/package:** use the relevant response guide in `govtribe-file-templates`, plus its `references/template-adaptation-and-revisions.md` for selected-file preservation and native format checks. Keep factual gaps and remaining file work separate from external copy; inline requests need inline text.
- **Find supporting files:** [Search_User_Files](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-user-files-mcp-tool) and [Vector-store content retrieval](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/guides/vector-store-content-retrieval). Before a GovTribe search, read [Choose a search mode and write queries](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/guides/choose-a-search-mode-and-write-queries), unless already in context; alternatively retrieve that article by title through `Documentation` in `govtribe-for-agents`.
