# Official correspondence

Draft cover letters, submission emails, contracting-officer questions and related communications using the supplied sender, recipient role, purpose and context.

## Workflow

1. **Start with the requested communication.** Reuse the existing email or letter for a revision. Follow a prescribed subject or form; otherwise identify the procurement and purpose in the subject.
2. **Make the purpose clear.** Put the question or requested action first. For a clarification, cite the specific provision, explain the ambiguity and ask an answerable question. For a cover email, identify the actual package and prepared attachments.
3. **Preserve the substance.** Use supplied names, dates, contact details, prices, validity periods and conditions. Include required representations or acceptance wording only when supported. Keep the tone natural and professional; cut stock introductions, flattery and repeated context.
4. **Keep revisions scoped.** If asked to rewrite one sentence or paragraph, return that passage. Preserve its commercial meaning and qualifications; do not add services, availability, prior discussions or approvals. For review-only requests, give findings.
5. **Check the source and channel.** Reconcile provision references and attachment names. If the user wants email wording but the notice requires a question form or portal, supply the draft and note the required channel separately. Keep missing contact details or signatures in the handoff.

Use `govtribe-file-templates` for complete response packages and native file checks.
