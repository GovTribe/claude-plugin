# Research sources

Reviewed October 16, 2026. Consult the relevant source when a procedural rule needs verification; routine prose edits do not require this bibliography. The actual procurement and applicable agency deviations determine the requirements.

| Source | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [GSA: Respond to a solicitation](https://www.gsa.gov/sell-to-government/step-2-compete-for-a-contract/respond-to-a-solicitation) | Understand requirements, evaluation factors and delivery instructions before writing the response. |
| [Codified FAR 15.304](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/15.304) | Evaluation factors and relative importance. In the [RFO Part 15 model text](https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/far-part-deviation-guide/far-overhaul-part-15), this subject is 15.104; RFO 15.304 covers defective pricing data. Check the applicable agency version. |
| [Codified FAR 15.201](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/15.201) | RFI and industry exchanges. RFO RFI guidance is at 15.101(c), and the post-RFP contracting-officer inquiry role is at 15.201(c)(1). |
| [FAR Overhaul parts and agency deviations](https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/far-part-deviation-guide) | Determine the applicable revision and agency deviation before relying on a section number or procedural default. |
| [GSA: How to Respond to Pre-Award Notices](https://www.gsa.gov/system/files/Pre-Award%20Notices%20-%20508%20-%2008272021.pdf), pp. 2–3 | RFI/sources-sought purposes and capability feedback. Educational guidance from 2021; do not reuse its historical thresholds. |
| [GAO: QED Systems, B-423634](https://www.gao.gov/products/b-423634), Discussion | Example of a required plan not being supplied in the required place; other information did not cure the omission. |
| [18F TDRS RFQ](https://github.com/18F/tdrs-app-rfq/blob/main/Final-RFQ/FINAL-TDRS-software-development-RFQ.md), section 9 | Historical example of prescribed components, limits, forms and link rules, not a universal response format. |
| [Digital.gov: Writing](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/writing) and [Headings](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/design/headings) | Direct verbs, clear explanations and useful headings where the buyer permits them. |
| [Section508.gov: Email](https://www.section508.gov/create/email-messages/), [Documents](https://www.section508.gov/create/documents/) and [PDFs](https://www.section508.gov/create/pdfs/) | Format-specific authoring and accessibility checks, handled through native format capabilities and File Templates. |
| [FAR 52.215-1](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.215-1) | Amendments, receipt instructions, signatures and restricted-data markings when incorporated and applicable. |
| [Oregon OHCS supplier guide](https://www.oregon.gov/ohcs/procurement/Pages/how-it-works.aspx) | Local questions, forms, addenda and channels; an example, not a nationwide state/local rule. |
