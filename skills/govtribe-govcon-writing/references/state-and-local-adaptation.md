# State and local adaptation

1. Reuse the issuing entity's current solicitation/addenda, questions, forms, evaluation criteria and deadline/time zone.
2. Preserve local terminology, headings and delivery channel. Apply federal clauses, registrations or representations only when the procurement actually requires them; federal funding alone does not import the entire federal process.
3. Draft from the same supported company evidence and selected revision. Identify missing local forms or facts specifically; retain already accepted answers.

The [Oregon OHCS supplier guide](https://www.oregon.gov/ohcs/procurement/Pages/how-it-works.aspx) illustrates following local questions, addenda, forms and submission instructions. Use the issuing buyer's guidance for the actual response.
