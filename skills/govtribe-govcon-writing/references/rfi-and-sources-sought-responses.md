# RFI and sources-sought responses

Answer the buyer's market-research questions. Use `govtribe-file-templates` reference `references/rfi-sources-sought-response.md` for the complete response package and its attachments.

## Workflow

1. **Recover the questions and respondent's role.** Reuse the current notice, question IDs, limits, company evidence and requirement map. Use the supplied respondent role and scope, whether prime delivery, subcontracted service, product or another contribution. If no role is supplied, draft supported answers without assigning one; keep any requested role recommendation distinct from company facts.
2. **Answer each question directly.** Retain its numbering and order. Give the requested experience, method, capacity, availability, constraint or business fact. A capability statement supplements the answers; it does not replace them. For an approach question, explain proposed activities and results using supported roles; identify undecided responsibilities separately. Omit closing offers or availability claims unless supported by company evidence.
3. **Support the answer.** Use concise project evidence: actual performer and role, customer type, period, relevant work, scale and supported results. Distinguish demonstrated work from a proposed approach and current capacity from unconfirmed surge. Preserve a known experience gap; do not invent a teammate or delivery plan to cover it. Missing evidence alone does not establish a negative answer—see [Source authority and claims](source-authority-and-claims.md).
4. **Provide requested feedback.** When invited, explain how a suggested requirement, schedule, acquisition approach or pilot would affect delivery. Label it as industry feedback. Include requested prices, vehicles and certifications from supported inputs; the notice determines what belongs in the response.
5. **Check every answer against its question.** Confirm that it addresses the actual subject, retains material limitations and agrees with attachments. Draft the supported parts and identify specific missing facts separately. Preserve required fields and disclosures; do not turn an incomplete answer into an apparently finished response.

## Surveys and short fields

- Count the final field value using the buyer's word/character convention, including spaces when specified. Use the actual notice or form's limits.
- Preserve actor, scope, capacity, timing and qualifications when shortening. Remove repetition before substance; add no new claims to make the answer sound stronger.
- Return plain text when requested, without labels or commentary inside the field. Keep internal follow-up questions separate.
