# Conduct Black Hat Review

## 1. Identify the opportunity and our team

Reuse the resolved opportunity and company context. For a focused question, assess only the relevant competitors and factors. For a supplied pursuit, use [Search_Pursuits](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-pursuits-mcp-tool) to retrieve its linked opportunity. Otherwise use [Search_Federal_Contract_Opportunities](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-federal-contract-opportunities-mcp-tool), [Search_Federal_Grant_Opportunities](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-federal-grant-opportunities-mcp-tool), or [Search_State_And_Local_Contract_Opportunities](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-state-and-local-contract-opportunities-mcp-tool), using `govtribe_ids` for known records.

Resolve our company with [Search_Vendors](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-vendors-mcp-tool); reuse its capabilities, proposed team and approach. Ask for missing context that could change the comparison. Without our company context, assess the competitor field and identify what is needed to tailor our response.

## 2. Read the buyer's current evaluation criteria

Use [Search_Government_Files](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-government-files-mcp-tool) for the solicitation or funding notice, amendments and Q&A. Reuse documents already read; compare file IDs, posting dates and amendment text to establish the controlling requirements. Read full files with native tools or [source-content retrieval](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/guides/vector-store-content-retrieval) when snippets are insufficient.

Identify scope, eligibility, vehicle access, deadlines, mandatory requirements, evaluation factors, relative importance and price/budget basis. Apply the notice's actual method for federal contracts, grants or state/local work. For early notices, distinguish stated requirements from provisional evaluation assumptions.

## 3. Establish the credible competitor field

Reuse [Likely Bidders](likely-bidders.md) and, for federal contracts, [Find Incumbent](find-incumbent-for-federal-contract-opportunity.md). Carry forward actual bidding entities, relevant awards, IDV/vehicle access, potential teammates and participation signals. Investigate gaps affecting the comparison rather than repeat completed discovery. Include credible challengers as well as the incumbent; relevant capability alone does not establish an intention to bid.

## 4. Anticipate each competitor's approach

For each serious competitor, develop its strongest plausible solution, staffing and transition approach, teaming arrangement and selling points. Use relevant awards, company websites, public announcements and supplied material. Reuse [Past Performance Match](past-performance-match.md) to assess comparable work, performing roles and documented results.

Explain which buyer priorities each approach could address and where delivery, access or experience gaps remain. An incumbent might emphasize continuity; a challenger might offer specialist expertise or a simpler delivery model. Distinguish documented plans from inferred strategies and identify the missing information that would change the assessment.

## 5. Compare competitive positions

Compare the likely approaches and ours against the buyer's factors. Identify meaningful advantages, shortcomings and risks, including how competitors could challenge our claims. Use the stated relative importance; under LPTA, assess acceptability and evaluated price rather than reward extra features.

Reuse [Capture Price-to-Win Review](conduct-price-to-win-review.md) when contract pricing affects the comparison; it routes detailed research to Pricing. For grants, apply the funding notice's budget and cost-sharing criteria. If a related selection dispute could clarify the comparison, use [Search_GAO_Bid_Protests](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-gao-bid-protests-mcp-tool) and [GAO Bid Protest Evidence](gao-bid-protest-evidence.md). Identify unsupported competitor-price or performance assumptions.

## 6. Recommend how to respond

Recommend changes tied to the competitive findings: strengthen transition, select better experience, fill a staffing gap, change teaming, revise pricing or sharpen a buyer-valued differentiator. Explain the expected benefit, feasibility, cost or margin implications and next action. Use [Bid / No-Bid Review](conduct-bid-no-bid-review.md) if the findings change whether or how to pursue.

Prepare an appropriate GovCon report explaining the competitive threats, recommended responses, supporting sources and unresolved questions. Let the requested use determine the format.
