# GovTribe Capture Workflows

Before your first GovTribe search, read [Choose a search mode and write queries](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/guides/choose-a-search-mode-and-write-queries), unless already in context. Alternatively, retrieve it with `Documentation` using `collections=["govtribe-for-agents"]` and `article_names=["Choose a search mode and write queries"]`.

## Research workflows

Choose references that match the request. For follow-ups, keep the active opportunity and company context. Tailor reports to their intended audience: internal capture teams, partners or government buyers.

- **Find opportunities for our company or pipeline:** [Relevant Opportunities](references/relevant-opportunities.md).
- **Analyze an opportunity or decide whether to bid:** [Bid / No-Bid Review](references/conduct-bid-no-bid-review.md).
- **Who is the incumbent?** [Find Incumbent](references/find-incumbent-for-federal-contract-opportunity.md) traces the performing contract or order.
- **Who might bid, and how do we compare?** [Likely Bidders](references/likely-bidders.md); [Black Hat Review](references/conduct-black-hat-review.md) for competitor strengths and countermoves.
- **Find or assess teaming partners:** Adapt [Likely Bidders](references/likely-bidders.md) discovery to complementary capabilities and buyer access; use [Past Performance Match](references/past-performance-match.md) to assess candidates against the workshare they would cover.
- **Which past performance fits this requirement?** [Past Performance Match](references/past-performance-match.md).
- **Expand into a federal buyer office:** [Federal Buyer Expansion Plan](references/federal-buyer-expansion-plan.md).
- **Create or manage pipelines and pursuits:** [Manage Pipelines and Pursuits](references/workspace-pipeline-operations.md).
- **Review pipeline progress and missing opportunities:** [Review My Pipeline](references/review-my-pipeline.md).

## Supporting references

- [Team On An Opportunity](references/team-on-an-opportunity.md): Use GovTribe teaming interests, match requests and team management.
- [Set Up Recurring Checks](references/relevant-opportunities.md#5-set-up-recurring-checks): Save useful searches or update existing monitors and their email cadence.
- [Price-to-Win Review](references/conduct-price-to-win-review.md): Apply pricing evidence from `govtribe-pricing-data` to pursuit decisions.
- [GAO Bid Protest Evidence](references/gao-bid-protest-evidence.md): Assess protest-related pursuit risk.
- **Existing company and proposal files:** [Search_User_Files](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-user-files-mcp-tool) for discovery; [Vector-store content retrieval](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/guides/vector-store-content-retrieval) for supporting passages. Use native file tools for originals or unsupported formats.

Use `govtribe-file-templates` for template masters and revision checks, `govtribe-govcon-writing` for narrative, and the host’s native capabilities for file creation and editing. Use `govtribe-proposal-workflows` to prepare or revise a requested response, including RFIs and sources-sought responses.
