# GovTribe Market Intelligence

Before your first GovTribe search, read [Choose a search mode and write queries](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/guides/choose-a-search-mode-and-write-queries), unless already in context. Alternatively, retrieve it with `Documentation` using `collections=["govtribe-for-agents"]` and `article_names=["Choose a search mode and write queries"]`.

## Research workflows

For **“deep dive this record”**, reuse the selected page, record ID or supplied URL and choose the matching reference below. Keep the requested audience and question in view; combine references when related records help answer it.

Different awards can share vendors; keep unverified roles and eligibility unresolved. Analog vendors can remain leads without predecessor proof.

- **Company deep dive:** [Company Profiles and Diligence](references/company-profiles-and-diligence.md) covers identity, ownership, capabilities, customers and government-business relationships.
- **Federal buyer or contact deep dive, spending patterns and market size:** [Federal Buying Pattern Analysis](references/federal-buying-pattern-analysis.md) covers agencies, offices, contracting contacts, contracts, grants, subawards and purchasing channels.
- **State or local buyer deep dive:** [State and Local Buying Pattern Analysis](references/state-local-buying-pattern-analysis.md) covers statewide activity and individual jurisdictions.
- **Federal opportunity or forecast deep dive:** [Federal Procurement Status and History](references/federal-procurement-status-and-history.md) traces notices, amendments, award outcomes and forecast progression.
- **Federal award, order, IDV or vehicle deep dive:** [Federal Contract and Vehicle Research](references/federal-contract-and-vehicle-research.md) explains contract history, holders, ordering terms and actual usage.
- **Grant opportunity, award or program deep dive:** [Grant Funding Research](references/grant-funding-research.md) covers funding terms, eligibility, recipients and program relationships.
- **State/local procurement deep dive:** [State and Local Procurement Research](references/state-and-local-procurement-research.md) follows opportunities, awards, IDVs and vehicles through original sources and related records.
- **Defense program deep dive:** [Defense Program and Supplier Research](references/defense-program-and-supplier-research.md) maps program activity, contracts and supported supplier relationships.
- **Upcoming federal demand:** [Early Federal Procurement Signals](references/find-early-federal-procurement-signals.md) covers expiring contracts, forecasts, contract and grant opportunities, and funding signals.
- **Future federal contract spending:** [Spending Forecast](references/spending-forecast.md) estimates obligations and timing from historical spending and known procurement changes.
- **Follow-on work for an existing opportunity, award, IDV or vehicle:** [Find Federal Recompete Opportunities](references/find-federal-recompete-opportunities.md) finds successor forecasts, contract opportunities or grant competitions.
- **Acquisition targets:** [Acquisition Target Scan](references/acquisition-target-scan.md) builds a vendor universe from government-market evidence.
- **Work held by firms nearing certification expiration:** [SBA Certification Expiration Analysis](references/sba-certification-graduation-dashboard.md) finds incumbent contracts and potential follow-on pursuits.

Use `govtribe-capture-workflows` for opportunity shortlists, company fit and pursuit decisions; `govtribe-pricing-data` for price/cost research; and `govtribe-proposal-workflows` for response preparation. Carry the resolved target and useful findings into those workflows.

## Supporting references

- [Federal Budget Data](references/federal-budget-data.md): Analyze funding changes using official OMB and DoD tables, agency appendices and detailed justifications, downloaded as needed.
- [Saved searches](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/guides/use-saved-searches): Reuse searches and set up email alerts. For recurring market reports, compare with the previous run; the first run establishes the baseline. Schedule recurring analysis through the host when requested.
- For a requested document, use `govtribe-file-templates` when a suitable master exists, `govtribe-govcon-writing` for narrative and the host's native format capabilities.
