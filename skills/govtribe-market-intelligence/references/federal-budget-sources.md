## Reading the tables

- **OMB:** filter the original workbook without dropping account or classification columns. Amounts are USD thousands, including negative offsetting receipts. FY2027's database has actuals through FY2025 and estimates for FY2026–FY2031; FY2025's has actuals through FY2023 and estimates for FY2024–FY2029. Keep budget authority and outlays separate. Match agency, bureau, account, subfunction, BEA category, on/off-budget status and, for outlays, grant/non-grant classification; check reorganizations across cycles. FY2027 adds a CGAC agency code column. The grant/non-grant flag identifies grants to state/local governments; it does not classify all spending as grants versus contracts.
- **DoD:** use `Exhibit P-1` and `Exhibit R-1`; skip display-subtotal rows when aggregating. Other workbook tabs repeat the same fiscal-year slices. Amounts are USD thousands; procurement quantities are separate. Preserve the column labels for actuals, enacted funding, spend plans, requests and totals. A blank remains unspecified, not zero. Sum P-1 rows marked `Add`, retaining cost type, and R-1 rows marked `Y` in `Include In TOA`; exclude non-additive detail. Do not add funding components to their total columns.

## Official sources

For cross-agency work, use the [OMB budget hub](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/information-resources/budget/), [agency appendices](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/information-resources/budget/appendix/), [historical tables](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/information-resources/budget/historical-tables/), [mid-session review](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/information-resources/budget/mid-session-review/) and [GovInfo prior budgets](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/collection/budget). Check the relevant fiscal year in [Congress's appropriations status tables](https://www.congress.gov/crs-appropriations-status-table), then read the applicable enacted text and explanatory statement.

For DoD program detail: [Army](https://www.asafm.army.mil/Budget-Materials/), [Navy/Marine Corps](https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Pages/default.aspx), [Air Force/Space Force](https://www.saffm.hq.af.mil/FM-Resources/Budget/), and [defense-wide books](https://comptroller.war.gov/Budget-Materials/FY2027BudgetJustification/). The central DoD hub also links O-1 for operations/maintenance and C-1 for construction.
