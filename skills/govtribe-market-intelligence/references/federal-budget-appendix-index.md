# FY2027 OMB Budget Appendix

The FY2027 Appendix is the President’s account-level request snapshot, with agency and branch schedules, proposed appropriations language, and supporting explanations. Download the relevant chapter below; use the [official Appendix page](https://www.whitehouse.gov/omb/information-resources/budget/appendix/) for newer releases or moved links. Use table labels to distinguish actual, enacted, and estimated amounts. Amounts are commonly in millions of dollars; OMB database workbooks use thousands. Search/extract relevant pages with native PDF tools. Text extraction can flatten columns, so use layout-preserving extraction or inspect relevant pages before aligning amounts with fiscal years. Full congressional justifications remain linked in [Federal Budget Sources](./federal-budget-sources.md).
