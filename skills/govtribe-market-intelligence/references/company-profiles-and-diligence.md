# Company Profiles and Diligence

## 1. Resolve the organization and requested scope

Reuse the selected company and the user's purpose. Use [Search_Vendors](https://govtribe.com/docs/govtribe-for-agents/tools/search-vendors-mcp-tool) to resolve legal name, UEI/CAGE, DBA, division, location and parent relationships. Establish whether the question concerns one registration, a business unit or a corporate family; keep namesakes separate. The researched organization is not automatically the user's company.

Check company websites, filings, sponsor portfolio pages and acquisition announcements for current ownership and affiliations. Record the source and date of a material change. Award `vendor_ids` can match direct and ultimate awardees: inspect `awardee` and `parent_of_awardee` before combining subsidiaries or reporting company totals. Follow [vendor and awardee roles](https://govtribe.com/docs/data-model/guides/vendor-awardee-recipient-and-subcontractor-roles).

## 2. Establish capabilities and government-business activity

Read company descriptions, supplied capability materials and representative awarded scopes. Distinguish registered classifications and company claims from supported products, services and performed work. An award establishes awarded scope; delivery or acceptance claims need further evidence.

Use the relevant portions of [Federal Buying Pattern Analysis](federal-buying-pattern-analysis.md) for customers, categories, prime contracts, grants, subcontract relationships, vehicles and changes over time. Keep these channels separate. Use [State and Local Buying Pattern Analysis](state-local-buying-pattern-analysis.md) when that market matters, matching source-provided company names against geography and other identifiers before attributing awards.

For a material contract or vehicle, use [Federal Contract and Vehicle Research](federal-contract-and-vehicle-research.md). Distinguish holding a vehicle from winning orders, and ceilings from obligations, revenue or backlog.

## 3. Investigate the relationships that matter

Trace reported prime/subcontractor relationships through the underlying awards and subawards using Federal Buying Pattern Analysis. Verify claimed partnerships, product authorizations and supplier roles against dated source documents. A shared customer, similar capability or award on the same program does not establish a business relationship.

Use current official announcements and company sources to investigate requested ownership, leadership, protest, partnership or market-access changes. Attribute source claims about headcount, revenue and financial condition; government award amounts cannot supply missing company financials. For a named person's procurement role, use [contact research](federal-buying-pattern-analysis.md#when-research-starts-from-a-contact).

## 4. Explain the company in the context of the question

Connect the strongest evidence to the requested profile or diligence issue, keeping dated facts, company claims and inferred implications distinguishable. Identify the missing source that would resolve a material uncertainty. Qualitative milestones are dated events, not a measured growth curve.

Use [Acquisition Target Scan](acquisition-target-scan.md) when comparing companies against an acquisition thesis. Use `govtribe-capture-workflows` when assessing a company as a bidder or teammate against a requirement, and `govtribe-pricing-data` for substantive rate or price comparisons.
